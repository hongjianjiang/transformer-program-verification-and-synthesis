## Interpretation

- 0/192 models achieve 100% on all three evaluated splits. Fifteen achieve 100% on both held-out sets, all from mono_word(4), but each has known training errors.
- More data did not consistently improve generalization within the fixed update budget. This does not establish that extra data cannot help: bigger datasets get fewer passes, and unique mixtures also change the sampling distribution.
- Corrected hard-loss gradients did not consistently improve outcomes. The ablation does not isolate whether architecture, optimization or curriculum is now the dominant issue.
- no2a(4), legacy original512: 100% mean original-test accuracy but only 51.4% diagnostic accuracy, revealing a major test-distribution limitation.
- Y(4) and Y(8) remain near chance in most ablation conditions.
- Earlier 26-language sweep: 208/208 configurations retrieved, zero model/extraction disagreements; 18/26 languages have at least one configuration perfect on training and the original test set. These are not formal equivalence certificates.
- Extended dot(16) and dot(24) runs both completed, each at 59.94% training and 50% test accuracy.
